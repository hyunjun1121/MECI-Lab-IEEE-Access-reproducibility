# IEEE Access Reference Evidence Matrix

Audit date: 2026-09-16

## Scope and rule

This matrix covers the external factual, conceptual, methodological, and
software claims in `main.tex` and `appendix.tex`. Claims generated by this
study are not assigned an external citation: their evidence is the frozen
analysis manifest, the named table or figure, or the persisted result files.
The abstract remains citation-free to comply with the local IEEE Access
submission guidance; its method and result sentences are study-specific.

An external citation is retained only when the cited source directly supports
the nearby clause. A source is not used to launder a broad claim that the
source does not establish. Model outputs, platform observations, and
interpretations are kept conceptually separate.

## Own-result coverage

The following blocks contain only results from the frozen YugsaTV/KFN result
sets, the persisted analysis manifests, or bounded interpretations of those
results. They intentionally do not receive external scientific citations:

- Abstract: all counts, associations, errors, transfer results, and the
  workflow conclusion are study outputs or scope statements.
- Data and Study Design: catalog counts, inclusion/exclusion counts, dates,
  time zone, privacy handling, and the KFN auxiliary frame are internal data
  provenance.
- Results: Sections on retention structure, cortical PCA, associations,
  incremental prediction, event review candidates, and cross-channel transfer
  are supported by Figures 2--7, Tables 1--3, and the frozen analysis files.
- Discussion and Conclusion: the interpretation of those findings, the
  prospective-study recommendation, and the limitations are bounded inferences
  from the stated design and are not presented as literature facts.
- Appendix B/C: null means, matched controls, sensitivity values, nonlinear
  metrics, and reproducibility settings are persisted audit outputs.

## Unsupported-claim resolutions

1. The abstract opening was rewritten from a general claim about what
   short-form platforms provide to a study-specific statement about evaluating
   a time-indexed representation. No citation is needed in the abstract.
2. The broad list of uncontrolled production and audience factors in the
   transfer discussion was replaced with the narrower, design-supported fact
   that the two channel analyses differ in context and available outcomes.
3. The unused false-discovery-rate entry was removed because the manuscript
   does not claim an FDR-controlled testing procedure.

## Citation integrity policy

Every cited key in the manuscript appears in `references.bib`, every
scientific BibTeX entry is cited in the manuscript, and DOI/URL uniqueness and
required metadata are checked by
`scripts/audit_ieee_access_references.py`. Source verification details and
access dates are recorded in `reference_search_log.md`.
